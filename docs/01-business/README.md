# Business documentation

Nhom nay mo ta `why`: van de nghiep vu, gia tri va quy tac doc lap voi cach
he thong duoc code.

## Noi dung can co

- `vision-and-scope.md`: muc tieu, KPI, in-scope va out-of-scope.
- `stakeholders.md`: vai tro, trach nhiem va quyen quyet dinh.
- `glossary.md`: thuat ngu va dinh nghia dung chung.
- `<feature>-process.md`: as-is/to-be flow, actor va exception flow.
- `<feature>-business-rules.md`: cac `RULE-*`, input va ket qua.
- `<feature>-requirements.md`: cac `BR-*`, priority va business acceptance.

## Khong dat tai day

- Endpoint/request/response: dat trong `04-api/`.
- Bang va field database: dat trong `05-data/`.
- Chi tiet component/framework: dat trong `03-architecture/`.

## Review owner

Product Owner/BA chiu trach nhiem chinh. Tech Lead, QA va stakeholder nghiep
vu review cac phan anh huong den kha thi va acceptance criteria.
