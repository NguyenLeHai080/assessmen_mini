# Operations documentation

Nhom nay giup team phat hien, chan doan va xu ly van de sau deployment.

## Noi dung can co

- `observability.md`: log, metric, trace va correlation ID.
- `alerts.md`: signal, threshold, severity, owner va runbook link.
- `runbooks/`: cach chan doan va khac phuc tung tinh huong.
- `incident-response.md`: role, communication, escalation va timeline.
- `service-levels.md`: SLI/SLO/SLA va error budget neu ap dung.
- `backup-restore.md`: schedule, retention va restore drill.

## Moi runbook phai co

- Symptom/alert va anh huong nguoi dung.
- Dashboard/log/query can xem.
- Safe diagnostic steps, mitigation va rollback.
- Khi nao escalation, owner/contact role va cach xac minh recovery.
- Link den incident/RCA lien quan; khong ghi credential.
